紬 Tsumugi — one rule per element

Reference sheet for docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-polish-pass-design.md. Each box shows what the app does today on the left and the agreed rule on the right. Everything on the right is live: hover it, and use the button above to see it in dark. Nothing here changes layout, widths or animation; those come later.

1 · Buttons

Four kinds and three sizes replace the eleven styles in use. Corners are 8px; small buttons 4px. Hover never changes what a button means: plain buttons get a pale wash, filled buttons go one step lighter.

7 · Titles, notes and messages

Titles in sentence case; a card inside a card gets a smaller title. Notes are upright grey text (no italics, no indigo); the finest print is one step smaller. Errors look like errors. Text lines stop at about 72 characters in every card.
